OUR SERVICES

From the front desk to follow-up

See a clinician

Start at reception for a hospital card. A nurse takes your vital signs before your consultation.

Get tests and medication

Your clinician sends requests to the lab or pharmacy. Results and dispensing updates return to your care record.

Plan ongoing care

Your care team can review earlier visits and record maternity, surgical and follow-up notes.
